05 Place and Route

From floorplan to clock tree synthesis, routing and GDS output.

Background: what are place and route and a clock tree?

A netlist is a connection table saying which part connects to which; it has no position information. Place and route (P&R) puts the parts on the chip and connects them with metal wires.

  • Floorplan: decide the size of the chip (block) and the positions of the pins.
  • Power wiring: lay thick wires that bring electricity to every part.
  • Placement: put the parts down. Parts that are strongly connected are placed close, which shortens wires.
  • Clock tree synthesis (CTS): build a tree of buffers that delivers the clock to all registers at almost the same time.
  • Routing: connect the parts with metal.

The longer a wire, the slower it is, so this is where the real speed first becomes visible. In the end, GDS, the drawing data to hand to the factory, is written out.

The tool is Synopsys IC Compiler II (icc2_shell). It gives the netlist its real shape. Only with place and route do wire delay, the clock tree and the power grid appear.

Overview of the steps

  1. Create the library and read the netlist (create_lib, read_verilog, link_block)
  2. RC models and scenario: parasitic models, and a scenario that reads the SDC (setup and hold are both analysed)
  3. Floorplan: core size and pin placement (initialize_floorplan, place_pins)
  4. Power grid: vertical power straps, the cell-row power rails and the vias between them (create_pg_*, compile_pg)
  5. Placement: place_opt places the cells and optimises while watching timing
  6. Clock tree synthesis + hold fixing: clock_opt
  7. Routing: route_auto, route_opt, check_routes
  8. Fillers fill the gaps
  9. Output: netlists (for verification), GDS, DEF, SDF

Floorplan

Rule of thumb: total cell area ÷ target utilisation (about 50% here) ≈ core area. Too small and it cannot be routed; too large and the wires get long.

Clock tree synthesis (CTS)

At placement time the clock is ideal (it reaches every flip-flop at the same moment). clock_opt builds a tree of clock buffers and evens out the arrival times at the flip-flops (the skew). At the same time it fixes hold violations, where data arrives too early, by inserting delay cells.

With a tiny circuit, CTS does nothing
With about 20 flip-flops the clock port can drive them all directly and no buffers are inserted. This course uses four lanes so that you can see a tree being built.

Look at the result

So that you can check the result without a layout viewer, there is a script that draws pictures from the GDS and DEF (tools/render_layout.py, run python3 tools/render_layout.py).

What to look at in the reports

Drawing power is not enough
If the power rails are not connected to the upper power straps by vias, timing and DRC still pass, but only LVS fails. Right after building the power grid, check that vias were added to every rail (in this course we really tripped over exactly this).
ICC2 results are provisional
The parasitic extraction inside ICC2 is simple. The final judgement is made in the following chapters with StarRC + PrimeTime.
